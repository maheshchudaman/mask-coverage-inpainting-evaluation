# Frozen ADE20K mask archive

`results/learned/frozen_masks_250x2x5/` contains NPZ shards holding 2,500
packed binary evaluation masks for the 250-image public-model study. It
contains no ADE20K photographs and no RGB image pixels. Each record stores an
ADE20K validation identifier, mask family, target and realised coverage, and a
SHA-256 digest. `manifest.json` records the size and SHA-256 digest of every
shard and the digest of the original combined archive.

The free-form masks were generated by the study code. The segmentation masks
were derived from ADE20K annotations. ADE20K states that its annotations and
software are licensed under the BSD 3-Clause terms. In accordance with those
